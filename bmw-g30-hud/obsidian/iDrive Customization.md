# iDrive Customization — the four roads

"Adjust iDrive" means very different things with very different risk.

| Road | What you get | Risk | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. **Coding** | Turn on features already in the firmware | Low, reversible | [[iDrive Coding Guide]] |
| 2. **Factory gauges on screen** | BMW's Sport displays | Low (it's coding) | [[Coding Tweaks Catalog]] → Displays & HUD |
| 3. **Custom gauges** | Your own live dashboard | Low on **phone** | [[Gauges Dashboard]] |
| 4. **Reskin / native apps** | Themes, games, apps on the iDrive screen | **High — bricks** | [[Head Unit Unlock Briefing]] |

## Key corrections
- **CarPlay can't draw custom gauges.** Apple only allows nav/audio/EV-type
  CarPlay templates — no third-party "dashboard" surface. So gauges *on the
  iDrive screen* = factory Sport displays (coding) or a head-unit app (road 4).
- The impressive "PlayStation" iDrive clips (custom dashboards + games on the
  center screen) are **unlocked head units** — road 4, not a coding toggle.

## What's safe and shipped
Roads 1–3. The live dashboard ([[Gauges Dashboard]]) gives most of the "M Vitals"
look, safely, on the phone — and it's more than BimmerCode can do.
