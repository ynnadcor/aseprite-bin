# BMW G30 Project — Home

A map of this project: making a 2018 BMW 540i (G30, B58, **NBT EVO / iDrive 6**)
more customizable, safely. Everything is **read-only to the car** unless a change
is applied by hand in BimmerCode. Nothing here writes engine tunes, touches
safety systems, or unlocks the head unit.

> This vault is public-safe: no VIN, serial numbers, MAC, or personal account
> details. Keep it that way if you add notes.
