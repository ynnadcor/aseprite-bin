# iDrive Coding Guide (app tab)

A guide to reversible iDrive coding changes for a G30 / NBT EVO. The app
**reads** your coding but never writes it — **you** apply each change in
**BimmerCode** (which you already have).

## How to use
1. Open the **iDrive** tab, pick a category, tap a change.
2. It shows: what it does, where to find it in BimmerCode, your car's
   requirements, caveats, and how to undo it.
3. It cross-checks your last [[Fault Code Reader]] scan to confirm your car
   answered for that module.
4. Make the change in BimmerCode.

## Good to know
- Availability depends on your car; **BimmerCode is the source of truth** for
  what your exact car/software supports.
- Everything is reversible.
- The full list: [[Coding Tweaks Catalog]].
